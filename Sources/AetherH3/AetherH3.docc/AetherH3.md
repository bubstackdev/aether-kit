# ``AetherH3``

CPU contracts for qualifying MiniMax H3 text-to-video-and-audio stages.

## Overview

> Important: Experimental and qualification only. These types describe probe plans and reference comparisons for native gates; they do not generate media.

Geometry, packing, the Turbo8 schedule, export-header identity, tensor files and the probe plans consumed by `AetherH3CoreAI`.
