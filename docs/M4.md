# M4: AetherVision and AetherEmbeddings (plan)

Status: **plan**, 2026-11-06, for after the 0.1.0 tag, with the owner's decisions of that day (at the end). M4a, the
vision foundation, is implemented. DESIGN.md §14 gives M4's exit criterion as "detector, depth, CLIP and embedder
parity gates". This plan says how to get there in PR-sized slices, like G0–G5 in
[INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md).

It's built from:
- [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) §8 (vision and embeddings) and §6 (runtime);
- [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md) §1.3 and §2.5–§2.9;
- the zoo contract survey `lab/contracts/vision-embeddings.md` and its drafts in `lab/contracts/manifests/`;
- the coreai-kit walkthrough `_reference/coreai-kit-architecture.md` §3.3;
- 0.0.x's `Sources/AetherVision` in `_reference/aether-kit-remote`.

## Scope

**In M4:** stateless, fully static graphs.

| Task | Driver (MANIFEST §1.3) | First model | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depth | `depth-estimator@1` | depth-anything-3-small | AetherVision |
| Detection, dense | `detector-dense@1` | yolox-s | AetherVision |
| Detection, DETR (+ split, seg) | `detector-detr@1` | rf-detr (nano; split and seg-nano as extras) | AetherVision |
| Image ↔ text embedding | `clip-joint@1` | clip-vit-b32 | AetherVision |
| Text embedding | `text-embedder@1` | embeddinggemma-300m | AetherEmbeddings |
| Zero-shot classification | `gliner-classifier@1` | gliner2.5-decide | AetherEmbeddings |
| Yes/no classifier, reranker | `yes-no-classifier@1` | shieldstral-3b | AetherEmbeddings |
| Visual document retrieval | `late-interaction-retriever@1` | colmodernvbert | AetherEmbeddings |
| Super-resolution | `image-to-image-tiled@1` | adcsr-x4 | AetherVision |

The exit criterion needs the first four rows plus the text embedder. The rest are optional M4 slices: each can slip
without blocking the milestone.

**Also in M4, after M4f:** VLM and OCR (`vlm-static-inject@1`, `vlm-embeds@1`, the `vision-tower@1` graph kind), as
later slices on M4a's patchify layouts and torch-parity bicubic.

**Not in M4:**
- Decision towers: M5 (AetherDecide).
- The one-call `Aether` ops: M6.

## What already exists

- **Manifest drivers and blocks (MANIFEST §1.3, §2):**
  - All nine drivers are already in `DriverCatalog.drivers`.
  - None has a `DriverContract` yet; `Sources/AetherCore/Manifest/DriverContract.swift` defines contracts only for
    `decoder-lm`, the image drivers and `kokoro`.
  - `preprocess.image`, `postprocess` and `labels` are typed and validated since M4a (`ManifestVision.swift`); each
    driver slice adds its `postprocess` interpreter.
  - `graphs.<g>.compute`, `buckets`, `source.from` (chaining), `fill: ones`, `tables` and `tokenizer.text` are already
    normative and in use.
- **Bundles:** every M4 model comes from our own Forge export (exporter, CPU reference and gate, as G1–G5), hosted
  under `aether-models` and pinned in the catalog like the generative entries. The zoo bundles in the survey are a
  reference for each model's I/O and decode rules only; no `zoo-overrides.json` entries are added for them.
- **Placement:** the measured rule in `lab/contracts/SUMMARY.md` #2 is GPU for every class. ANE is allowed only with a
  measured win; gliner was 6× slower there, and CLIP produces NaN on the CPU.
- **Runtime (M0–G5):** M4 reuses all of these and adds no parallel ones:
  - `Graph`, `TensorArena` and `Bindings`;
  - `GraphResidency`, `FootprintLedger` and `ResidencyPlan` admission;
  - `ShapeProfile`;
  - `PipelineOptions` (AetherHost): memory, coordinator, GPU admission, ledger, and a probe that can be injected;
  - `Registry`;
  - `Tools/DeviceGate` for iPhone runs;
  - the Forge exporter shape with CPU-reference gates.

  The pre-release review fix for manifests without phases (their footprints are now read back from the ledger)
  matters here: every M4 graph is single-phase.

## Image preprocessing (the shared primitive) — done in M4a

`ImageInput` (the value) and `ImagePreprocessor` (the manifest-driven transform) producing `PreparedImage`, in
AetherRuntime so AetherVision, AetherEmbeddings and AetherDecide share them (DESIGN §8). Configured by the typed
`preprocess.image` (MANIFEST §2.5).
- **Decode:** 0.0.x's `VisionRGBImage`, ported with its tests: ImageIO decode, EXIF orientation, alpha policy
  (`dropAlpha`, Pillow's `convert("RGB")`, or `composite`), `deviceRGB` or `sRGB`. `CGImage` and URL inputs;
  `CVPixelBuffer` comes with the live-camera slice.
- **Resize:** `stretch`, `letterbox` (anchor, pad), `centerCrop`, `fitLongSide`. Filters: `vImage` (fast), and 0.0.x's
  antialiased bicubic moved into each reference's fixed-point arithmetic, as `pillowBicubic` and `torchUint8Bicubic`.
  TODO when an export needs them: `pillowBilinear`, `coreGraphicsHigh`, smart sizing (Qwen3-VL), tiling (AdcSR).
- **Normalize and lay out:** colour space, RGB/BGR, scale, mean and std; `NCHW`, `NVCHW`, `patches` (block-major
  `C,T,py,px` as Qwen-VL, row-major `py,px,C` as SigLIP 2); host dtype fp32 or fp16, converted at bind to the graph's.
- **Real-time path:** `prepare` (CPU) and the graph run (GPU) are separate calls by type.
- **Parity (measured):** fixtures from `Forge/vision/fixtures_preprocess.py` (Pillow 13.0, torch 2.11). The parity
  filters and nine whole blocks match bit for bit. `vImage`'s kernel is Accelerate's own, so the planned ±1 LSB holds
  for 99.6–99.96 % of values against Pillow's Lanczos on smooth images, with a maximum of 2; the suite pins those bars.

## Drivers and blocks to add

Each model slice is a Forge exporter (with its CPU reference and gate) plus a driver: a `DriverContract`, a typed
`postprocess` interpreter (MANIFEST §2.8, already decoded) and an actor on M4a's `StatelessPipeline`. The survey's "cannot express" items 1–13 are the checklist; most are already answered by MANIFEST §2.

| Driver | Postprocess (§2.8) | Notes from the survey |
|---|---|---|
| `depth-estimator@1` | `depth {polarity: largerIsFarther, resizeBack}` | DA3 takes fp16 [1,3,504,504] in raw [0,1] (ImageNet normalization is in the graph); `depth_conf` is optional, otherwise listed in `unusedOutputs` |
| `detector-dense@1` | `denseNms {boxFormat: cxcywh, boxSpace: letterboxPixels, nms: perClass 0.45}`, defaults score 0.3 and max 50 | BGR, 0–255, letterbox top-left with pad 114; `labels: {set: coco-91}`, built in, since coreai-kit hard-codes it |
| `detector-detr@1` | `detrSigmoid`, optional `masks` | the split variant chains `backbone.features` through `source.from` |
| `clip-joint@1` | `embedding {normalized, similarity: cosine}` | static text batch of 3 with unused rows (`tokenizer.text.unusedBatchRows`); CLIP's lowercase BPE reuses AetherImage's `CLIPTokenizer` |
| `text-embedder@1` | `embedding {pooling: inGraph}` | pad id 0, right, fixed 256; query and document prompts read from `reference.json` with `file#key` |
| `gliner-classifier@1` | `gliner` | sequence `buckets` s256/s512; marker ids from `classifier.json` |
| `yes-no-classifier@1` | `yesNo {positiveIndex, threshold}` | pad id 11 on the right; flattened prompt template; 2.5 GB graph |
| `late-interaction-retriever@1` | `maxSim {imageTokenSpan, patchGrid}` | two subtrees (`query/`, `doc/`); `pixelMask` filled with ones |
| `image-to-image-tiled@1` | `superRes {range: [-1,1], colorMatch: host}` | tiling with overlap `max(4, tile/8)` |

## Public API sketch

The style follows AetherImage and AetherSpeech:
- one actor per loaded model, which serializes requests (they queue, never fail);
- `load(bundle:manifest:device:options:)` plus `Registry.vision` and `Registry.embeddings`;
- AetherHost's `PipelineOptions`;
- cancellation between graph calls;
- typed `AetherError`s.

```swift
// AetherVision
public struct ImageInput: Sendable { init(_ cgImage: CGImage); init(contentsOf url: URL) }   // + CVPixelBuffer later
public actor DepthEstimator   { func estimate(_ image: ImageInput) async throws -> DepthMap }      // map + confidence?
public actor ObjectDetector   { func detect(_ image: ImageInput, _ request: DetectionRequest = .init()) async throws -> [Detection] }
public struct Detection: Sendable { var label: Label; var score: Float; var box: CGRect /* normalized, source space */; var mask: Mask? }
public actor ImageTextEncoder { func embed(image: ImageInput) async throws -> Embedding
                                func embed(texts: [String]) async throws -> [Embedding]
                                func classify(_ image: ImageInput, labels: [String]) async throws -> [LabelScore] }
public actor SuperResolver    { func upscale(_ image: ImageInput) async throws -> GeneratedImage }  // G0's type

// AetherEmbeddings
public struct Embedding: Sendable { var vector: [Float]; func similarity(_ other: Embedding) -> Float }
public actor TextEmbedder      { func embed(_ texts: [String], role: EmbeddingRole /* .query | .document */) async throws -> [Embedding] }
public actor TextClassifier    { func classify(_ text: String, labels: [String]) async throws -> [LabelScore] }   // GLiNER
public actor YesNoClassifier   { func score(_ pairs: [(query: String, document: String)]) async throws -> [Float] } // also rerank
public actor DocumentRetriever { func embed(query: String) async throws -> MultiVector
                                 func embed(page: ImageInput) async throws -> MultiVector }                 // + MaxSim score
```

Geometry is returned in normalized source-image space. Mapping onto a view stays with the caller; `LiveVision` (M4l)
adds an overlay helper.

## Validation

- **Reference per model.** A Forge reference script runs the upstream PyTorch model on the CPU on **the same
  preprocessed tensor**, so preprocessing and the graph are gated separately. The zoo's sidecar references
  (EmbeddingGemma's and Shieldstral's `reference.json`, GLiNER's `gate/`, ColModernVBERT's) are cross-checks.
- **Bars:**

  | Output | Bar |
  |---|---|
  | Embeddings | cosine ≥ 0.999 per vector |
  | Depth | rel L2 ≤ 0.02 |
  | Detection | same class set; matched boxes IoU ≥ 0.95, scores within 0.01 |
  | GLiNER and yes/no | probabilities within 0.01, same argmax |
  | Super-resolution | PSNR ≥ 40 dB against the reference |

  These are starting bars: confirm or tighten each from the first measurement. Never loosen one to pass.
- **Hermetic tests:** preprocessing known answers, NMS, box mapping, MaxSim, pooling rules and every `postprocess`
  interpreter, with fake graphs and a constant `GPUMemoryProbe`.
- **iPhone:** extend `Tools/DeviceGate` with each model that has an iOS variant (GLiNER ships `ios/` and `ios-h19p/`).
  Also measure ANE placement and power for the static vision graphs on the device. SUMMARY #2 leaves exactly that as
  the open M4 device task, and it decides whether any entry earns a `neuralEngine` override.

## What to port

- **From 0.0.x (trusted):** `VisionRGBImage` (decode, EXIF, alpha) and `Qwen3VLImagePreprocessor`'s bicubic resize and
  patchify, with their tests. 0.0.x has no detector, depth or embedder code; its vision code served the VLM path.
- **From coreai-kit:**
  - **Behaviour only, as reference:** each pipeline's decode rules. The survey already records them: YOLOX
    letterboxing and NMS, the DA3 rank-5 multi-view replication, CLIP's static batch, and the embedders' pad ids and
    prompts.
  - **Not ported:** its role detection by rank, name substring and **catalog-id string** (`KitDetector` "yolox-s",
    `VisualDocumentRetriever` "colmodernvbert"); its ~10 preprocessors; the hard-coded pad ids and COCO table; and
    `GraphModel`'s per-call allocation. The manifest and `Bindings` replace all of them.
