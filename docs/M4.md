# M4: AetherVision and AetherEmbeddings (plan)

Status: **plan**, 2026-11-06, for after the 0.1.0 tag, with the owner's decisions of that day (at the end). M4a, the
vision foundation, M4b, depth, M4c, text embedding, M4d, CLIP, M4e, dense detection, and M4f, DETR detection with masks, are implemented. DESIGN.md §14 gives M4's exit criterion as "detector, depth, CLIP and embedder
parity gates". This plan says how to get there in PR-sized slices, like G0–G5 in
[INTEGRATION.md](INTEGRATION.md).

It's built from:
- [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) §8 (vision and embeddings) and §6 (runtime);
- [MANIFEST.md](MANIFEST.md) §1.3 and §2.5–§2.9;
- the zoo contract survey `lab/contracts/vision-embeddings.md` and its drafts in `lab/contracts/manifests/`;
- the coreai-kit walkthrough `_reference/coreai-kit-architecture.md` §3.3;
- 0.0.x's `Sources/AetherVision` in `_reference/aether-kit-remote`.

## Scope

**In M4:** stateless, fully static graphs.

| Task | Driver (MANIFEST §1.3) | First model | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depth | `depth-estimator@1` | depth-anything-3-small | AetherVision |
| Detection, dense | `detector-dense@1` | yolox-s | AetherVision |
| Detection, DETR (+ split, seg) | `detector-detr@1` | rf-detr (nano; split and seg-nano as extras) | AetherVision |
| Image ↔ text embedding | `clip-joint@1` | clip-vit-b32 | AetherVision |
| Text embedding | `text-embedder@1` | embeddinggemma-300m | AetherEmbeddings |
| Zero-shot classification | `gliner-classifier@1` | gliner2.5-decide | AetherEmbeddings |
| Yes/no classifier, reranker | `yes-no-classifier@1` | shieldstral-3b | AetherEmbeddings |
| Visual document retrieval | `late-interaction-retriever@1` | colmodernvbert | AetherEmbeddings |
| Super-resolution | `image-to-image-tiled@1` | adcsr-x4 | AetherVision |

The exit criterion needs the first four rows plus the text embedder. The rest are optional M4 slices: each can slip
without blocking the milestone.

**Also in M4, after M4f:** VLM and OCR (`vlm-static-inject@1`, `vlm-embeds@1`, the `vision-tower@1` graph kind), as
later slices on M4a's patchify layouts and torch-parity bicubic.

**Not in M4:**
- Decision towers: M5 (AetherDecide).
- The one-call `Aether` ops: M6.

## What already exists

- **Manifest drivers and blocks (MANIFEST §1.3, §2):**
  - All nine drivers are already in `DriverCatalog.drivers`.
  - `Sources/AetherCore/Manifest/DriverContract.swift` defines contracts for `decoder-lm`, the image drivers, `kokoro`,
    `minimax-h3` and, since M4b, M4d, M4e and M4f, `depth-estimator`, `clip-joint`, `detector-dense` and
    `detector-detr`; the other M4 drivers get theirs in their slices.
  - `preprocess.image`, `postprocess` and `labels` are typed and validated since M4a (`ManifestVision.swift`); each
    driver slice adds its `postprocess` interpreter.
  - `graphs.<g>.compute`, `buckets`, `source.from` (chaining), `fill: ones`, `tables` and `tokenizer.text` are already
    normative and in use.
- **Bundles:** every M4 model comes from our own Forge export (exporter, CPU reference and gate, as G1–G5), hosted
  under `aether-models` and pinned in the catalog like the generative entries. The zoo bundles in the survey are a
  reference for each model's I/O and decode rules only; no `zoo-overrides.json` entries are added for them.
- **Placement:** the measured rule in `lab/contracts/SUMMARY.md` #2 is GPU for every class. ANE is allowed only with a
  measured win; gliner was 6× slower there, and CLIP produces NaN on the CPU.
- **Runtime (M0–G5):** M4 reuses all of these and adds no parallel ones:
  - `Graph`, `TensorArena` and `Bindings`;
  - `GraphResidency`, `FootprintLedger` and `ResidencyPlan` admission;
  - `ShapeProfile`;
  - `PipelineOptions` (AetherHost): memory, coordinator, GPU admission, ledger, and a probe that can be injected;
  - `Registry`;
  - `Tools/DeviceGate` for iPhone runs;
  - the Forge exporter shape with CPU-reference gates.

  The pre-release review fix for manifests without phases (their footprints are now read back from the ledger)
  matters here: every M4 graph is single-phase.

## Image preprocessing (the shared primitive) — done in M4a

`ImageInput` (the value) and `ImagePreprocessor` (the manifest-driven transform) producing `PreparedImage`, in
AetherRuntime so AetherVision, AetherEmbeddings and AetherDecide share them (DESIGN §8). Configured by the typed
`preprocess.image` (MANIFEST §2.5).
- **Decode:** 0.0.x's `VisionRGBImage`, ported with its tests: ImageIO decode, EXIF orientation, alpha policy
  (`dropAlpha`, Pillow's `convert("RGB")`, or `composite`), `deviceRGB` or `sRGB`. `CGImage` and URL inputs;
  `CVPixelBuffer` comes with the live-camera slice.
- **Resize:** `stretch`, `letterbox` (anchor, pad), `centerCrop`, `fitLongSide`. Filters: `vImage` (fast), and 0.0.x's
  antialiased bicubic moved into each reference's fixed-point arithmetic, as `pillowBicubic` and `torchUint8Bicubic`.
  TODO when an export needs them: `pillowBilinear`, `coreGraphicsHigh`, smart sizing (Qwen3-VL), tiling (AdcSR).
- **Normalize and lay out:** colour space, RGB/BGR, scale, mean and std; `NCHW`, `NVCHW`, `patches` (block-major
  `C,T,py,px` as Qwen-VL, row-major `py,px,C` as SigLIP 2); host dtype fp32 or fp16, converted at bind to the graph's.
- **Real-time path:** `prepare` (CPU) and the graph run (GPU) are separate calls by type.
- **Parity (measured):** fixtures from `Forge/vision/fixtures_preprocess.py` (Pillow 13.0, torch 2.11). The parity
  filters and nine whole blocks match bit for bit. `vImage`'s kernel is Accelerate's own, so the planned ±1 LSB holds
  for 99.6–99.96 % of values against Pillow's Lanczos on smooth images, with a maximum of 2; the suite pins those bars.

## Public API sketch

The style follows AetherImage and AetherSpeech:
- one actor per loaded model, which serializes requests (they queue, never fail);
- `load(bundle:manifest:device:options:)` plus `Registry.vision` and `Registry.embeddings`;
- AetherHost's `PipelineOptions`;
- cancellation between graph calls;
- typed `AetherError`s.

```swift
// AetherVision
public struct ImageInput: Sendable { init(_ cgImage: CGImage); init(contentsOf url: URL) }   // + CVPixelBuffer later
public actor DepthEstimator   { func estimate(_ image: ImageInput) async throws -> DepthMap }      // map + confidence?
public actor ObjectDetector   { func detect(_ image: ImageInput, _ request: DetectionRequest = .init()) async throws -> [Detection] }
public struct Detection: Sendable { var label: Label; var score: Float; var box: CGRect /* normalized, source space */; var mask: Mask? }
public actor ImageTextEncoder { func embed(image: ImageInput) async throws -> Embedding
                                func embed(texts: [String]) async throws -> [Embedding]
                                func classify(_ image: ImageInput, labels: [String]) async throws -> [LabelScore] }
public actor SuperResolver    { func upscale(_ image: ImageInput) async throws -> GeneratedImage }  // G0's type

// AetherEmbeddings
public struct Embedding: Sendable { var vector: [Float]; func similarity(_ other: Embedding) -> Float }
public actor TextEmbedder      { func embed(_ texts: [String], role: EmbeddingRole /* .query | .document */) async throws -> [Embedding] }
public actor TextClassifier    { func classify(_ text: String, labels: [String]) async throws -> [LabelScore] }   // GLiNER
public actor YesNoClassifier   { func score(_ pairs: [(query: String, document: String)]) async throws -> [Float] } // also rerank
public actor DocumentRetriever { func embed(query: String) async throws -> MultiVector
                                 func embed(page: ImageInput) async throws -> MultiVector }                 // + MaxSim score
```

Geometry is returned in normalized source-image space. Mapping onto a view stays with the caller; `LiveVision` (M4l)
adds an overlay helper.

## Validation

- **Reference per model.** A Forge reference script runs the upstream PyTorch model on the CPU on **the same
  preprocessed tensor**, so preprocessing and the graph are gated separately. The zoo's sidecar references
  (EmbeddingGemma's and Shieldstral's `reference.json`, GLiNER's `gate/`, ColModernVBERT's) are cross-checks.
- **Bars:**

  | Output | Bar |
  |---|---|
  | Embeddings | cosine ≥ 0.999 per vector |
  | Depth | rel L2 ≤ 0.02 |
  | Detection | same class set; matched boxes IoU ≥ 0.95, scores within 0.01 |
  | GLiNER and yes/no | probabilities within 0.01, same argmax |
  | Super-resolution | PSNR ≥ 40 dB against the reference |

  These are starting bars: confirm or tighten each from the first measurement. Never loosen one to pass.
- **Hermetic tests:** preprocessing known answers, NMS, box mapping, MaxSim, pooling rules and every `postprocess`
  interpreter, with fake graphs and a constant `GPUMemoryProbe`.
- **iPhone:** extend `Tools/DeviceGate` with each model that has an iOS variant (GLiNER ships `ios/` and `ios-h19p/`).
  Also measure ANE placement and power for the static vision graphs on the device. SUMMARY #2 leaves exactly that as
  the open M4 device task, and it decides whether any entry earns a `neuralEngine` override.

## What to port

- **From 0.0.x (trusted):** `VisionRGBImage` (decode, EXIF, alpha) and `Qwen3VLImagePreprocessor`'s bicubic resize and
  patchify, with their tests. 0.0.x has no detector, depth or embedder code; its vision code served the VLM path.
- **From coreai-kit:**
  - **Behaviour only, as reference:** each pipeline's decode rules. The survey already records them: YOLOX
    letterboxing and NMS, the DA3 rank-5 multi-view replication, CLIP's static batch, and the embedders' pad ids and
    prompts.
  - **Not ported:** its role detection by rank, name substring and **catalog-id string** (`KitDetector` "yolox-s",
    `VisualDocumentRetriever` "colmodernvbert"); its ~10 preprocessors; the hard-coded pad ids and COCO table; and
    `GraphModel`'s per-call allocation. The manifest and `Bindings` replace all of them.

## M4b: depth (done)

- **Driver:** `depth-estimator@1`'s `DriverContract` (`estimator`: in `image` rank 4, out `depth` rank 3, optional
  `confidence`), checked with `preprocess.image` (`NCHW`) and a `depth` `postprocess` before anything loads; each output
  must be `[1, H, W]` at the prepared image's size. `DepthEstimatorDriver` in `Registry.vision` loads a
  `DepthEstimatorModel`; its `estimator()` is the actor on `StatelessPipeline`.
- **Postprocess:** `resizeBack` resamples depth and confidence to the source through `ImageGeometry` (bilinear at pixel
  centres, as `F.interpolate(align_corners=False)` without antialiasing, clamped to the content rectangle so letterbox
  padding is never read); `DepthRequest.resizeBack` overrides the manifest. `DepthMap.polarity` is the manifest's: DA3
  is `largerIsFarther`, relative (not metric) depth.
- **Gate** (`DepthEstimatorGateTests`, M5 Max, macOS 27): on the GPU against the upstream model on the CPU in float32,
  same tensor, bar rel L2 ≤ 0.02:

  | Case | Depth rel L2 | Confidence rel L2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | soh-000 | 0.00074 | 0.00032 |
  | soh-010 | 0.00041 | 0.00028 |
  | synthetic-7 | 0.00020 | 0.00112 |

  The image cases rebuilt as 8-bit images and run from `ImageInput` give the same numbers (the identity stretch). The
  SOH source frames (1208 × 680) end to end, resized back: finite, the sky farther than the foreground, rel L2 0.011
  and 0.014 to the reference resized the same way (not gated: vImage versus Pillow's bicubic). Footprint: peak 358 MiB,
  held 343 MiB.
- **Catalog:** `depth-anything-3-small`, `aether-models/aether-kit-depth-anything-3-small` at `09600710`, macOS only.

## M4e: dense detection (done)

- **Driver:** `detector-dense@1`'s `DriverContract` (`detector`: in `image` rank 4, out `preds` rank 3), checked with
  `preprocess.image` (`NCHW`), a `denseNms` `postprocess` and `labels` before anything loads; `preds` must be
  `[1, A, 5 + C]` with C the label set's size for contiguous columns. `DenseDetectorDriver` in `Registry.vision` loads
  an `ObjectDetectorModel`; its `detector()` is the `ObjectDetector` actor on `StatelessPipeline`, which the DETR driver
  (M4f) shares.
- **Shared types** (`Detection.swift`): `Detection` (label `{id, name}`, score, box normalized in the source and clipped
  to it, `mask: DetectionMask?`), `DetectionRequest` (`scoreThreshold`, `maxDetections`, `job`, over the manifest's
  defaults) and `DetectionLabels` (`contiguous` or `cocoId` columns; `coco-91` built in: the 80 used ids, upstream's
  names).
- **Postprocess:** `denseNms` as MANIFEST.md §2.8 states it, upstream YOLOX's `postprocess` in Double; boxes map back
  through the letterbox's content rectangle. Upstream divides by the ratio r instead, which differs by under 0.2% of the
  image where `int(side · r)` floors (≤ 0.0019 normalized on the reference cases).
- **Manifest:** the export writes `padValue: 114`, a bare number; the typed manifest now accepts it as `[114]`.
- **Gate** (`ObjectDetectorDenseGateTests`, M5 Max, macOS 27): the host NMS on each reference `preds.f32` keeps exactly
  the reference's detections (anchors, classes, boxes; scores within 1e-12). On the GPU, each case's `input.f32`
  through the detector against the CPU float32 reference (bar: same class set, matched IoU ≥ 0.95, scores within 0.01):

  | Case | Detections | Min IoU | Max score Δ |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | dog | 5 / 5 | 1.00000 | 1e-6 |
  | demo (2443 × 424 strip) | 11 / 11 | 1.00000 | 1e-6 |
  | demo-traffic | 9 / 9 | 1.00000 | 2e-6 |
  | demo-soccer | 5 / 5 | 1.00000 | 1e-6 |
  | demo-bikes | 4 / 4 | 1.00000 | 1e-6 |
  | synthetic-7 | 0 / 0 | — | — |

  End to end from `ImageInput` (the upstream images, crops cut with `CGImage.cropping`; reported, not gated, because
  the kit letterboxes with vImage and the reference with Pillow's bilinear): dog 5/5 matched (IoU ≥ 0.98, scores within
  0.007, the dog and bicycle found), demo-traffic 9/9 and demo-soccer 5/5 (IoU ≥ 0.90, scores within 0.08), demo 9 of
  11 and demo-bikes 3 of 4 (low-score detections near the threshold differ). Upstream's float `int(2443 · r)` letterboxes
  the demo strip to 639 columns where the kit's integer floor gives 640. About 0.24 s a request, measured while sibling gates shared the GPU;
  footprint peak 371 MiB, held 361 MiB.
- **Catalog:** `yolox-s`, `aether-models/aether-kit-yolox-s` at `6a608906`, macOS only.

## M4c: text embedding (done)

- **Driver:** `text-embedder@1`'s `DriverContract` (`encoder`: in `tokens` and `attentionMask` rank 2 integer, out
  `embedding` rank 2), checked with a tokenizer, `tokenizer.text` (`padId`, `padTo` equal to
  `limits.staticSequence`) and an `embedding` `postprocess` (`pooling: inGraph`) before anything loads.
  `TextEmbedderDriver` in `Registry.embeddings` loads a `TextEmbedderModel`; its `embedder()` loads the tokenizer
  (AetherText's adapter, from the bundle) and is the actor on `StatelessPipeline`.
- **Tokenization** (`tokenizer.text`, typed as `Manifest.Tokenizer.Text`): `prompts[role]` + text, stripped, encoded
  with `<bos>` 2 … `<eos>` 1; longer than 256, it keeps 254 content ids and both specials; right-padded with 0; mask 1
  for every real id. `TokenizedText` reports the count and whether it was truncated.
- **`Embedding`** lives in AetherRuntime so AetherVision's CLIP vectors and AetherEmbeddings' share one type: `vector`,
  `dimension`, cosine `similarity(_:)` (M4d adds `dot`, `norm`, `normalized`); `truncated(to:)` re-normalizes a
  Matryoshka prefix (EmbeddingGemma: 512, 256, 128).
- **Gate** (`TextEmbedderGateTests`, M5 Max, macOS 27): the driver's own tokenization against the reference's ids
  (7/7 exact, masks too), then the GPU embedding against the upstream model on the CPU in float32, bar cosine ≥ 0.999:

  | Case | Tokens | Cosine | max abs diff |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | query-short | 18 | 0.99999994 | 5.6e-5 |
  | query-long | 36 | 0.99999994 | 5.9e-5 |
  | query-french | 23 | 0.99999994 | 4.9e-5 |
  | document-short | 25 | 0.99999994 | 4.2e-5 |
  | document-medium | 85 | 0.9999999 | 5.9e-5 |
  | document-near-256 | 252 | 0.9999999 | 6.0e-5 |
  | document-truncated | 317 → 256 | 0.9999999 | 6.5e-5 |

  Every query's best document is the reference's; the query × document cosine table is within 5.2e-5 of it. Repeats
  are bit-identical. About 11–20 ms per text once loaded; footprint peak 442 MiB, held 438 MiB.
- **Catalog:** `embeddinggemma-300m`, `aether-models/aether-kit-embeddinggemma-300m` at `d5016965`, macOS only,
  license `gemma`, not mature. The repo is gated with automatic approval (the owner's choice, like the Krea realism
  repo; Hugging Face's default gate prompt), so `gen-catalog` and `ModelStore` need a token.

## M4d: CLIP (done)

- **Driver:** `clip-joint@1`'s `DriverContract`: graph `imageEncoder` (in `image` rank 4, out `imageEmbedding` rank 2)
  and graph `textEncoder` (in `tokens` rank 2 integer and an optional `attentionMask`, out `textEmbedding` rank 2),
  checked with `preprocess.image` (`NCHW`), an `embedding` postprocess with `logitScale` and a `clip-bpe` tokenizer
  with a `text` block before anything loads. `ImageTextEncoderDriver` in `Registry.vision` loads an
  `ImageTextEncoderModel`; its `encoder()` is the actor on `StatelessPipeline`.
- **API:** `embed(image:)` (`ImageInput` or a `prepare(_:)`d `PreparedImage`), `embed(texts:)`,
  `classify(_:labels:template:)` → `[LabelScore]` in the labels' order (`softmax(logitScale · similarity)`, one GPU
  grant for the image and the label texts), `scores(image:labels:texts:)` for label embeddings made once,
  `tokenize(_:)`; `ImageTextRequest` carries the `GPUJob`.
- **Shared types:** M4c's `Embedding` in AetherRuntime, one type for both drivers (not in AetherEmbeddings as the
  sketch above had it: AetherVision must not depend on AetherEmbeddings, and both depend on AetherRuntime), gains `dot`,
  `norm` and `normalized` beside `vector`, `dimension` (M4c's `dimensions`, renamed) and cosine `similarity`;
  `LabelScore` joins it. `CLIPTokenizer` moved there from AetherImage for the same reason. `classify`'s template
  defaults to OpenAI's `"a photo of a {}."` (the owner's decision); `"{}"` embeds the labels as they are.
- **Text batches:** the text graph's `buckets` on axis `batch` (functions `batch1`, `batch8`); texts run in groups of
  8, each at the smallest bucket that holds it, unused rows `[BOS, EOS]` then the pad id, their outputs dropped. Each
  bucket is its own graph in the residency (`GraphResidency.graph(_:bucket:)`, key `textEncoder[8]`); bucket 1 is the
  graph's own function.
- **Tokenizer fix:** the added tokens come from the tokenizer files. SD 2.x's adds `!` as its pad (0); OpenAI CLIP's
  doesn't, so `hello!` is 3306, 256 (it was 3306, 0). SD-Turbo's tokenizer tests and gate are unchanged.
- **Manifest fields** the exporter writes, now typed and in MANIFEST.md: `resize.shortestSide`,
  `embedding.dimension`/`logitScale`, `buckets.axis: batch`, and in M4c's `Manifest.Tokenizer.Text`,
  `unusedBatchRows` (a row prefix) and the mask rules `nonPad`, `throughFirstEos` and `none` beside `realTokens` (the
  default; for a CLIP row the same as `throughFirstEos`). The CLIP driver requires `padId` and `padTo`.
- **Gate** (`ImageTextEncoderGateTests`, M5 Max, macOS 27): on the GPU (fp16 towers) against transformers'
  `CLIPModel` on the CPU in float32, same inputs, bar cosine ≥ 0.999:

  | Case | Image cosine | Zero-shot (reference argmax) | Logits max \|Δ\| |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | astronaut | 0.9999986 | astronaut 0.9996 ✓ | 0.0085 |
  | chelsea | 0.9999996 | cat 0.9937 ✓ | 0.0114 |
  | coffee | 0.9999983 | cup of coffee 0.9998 ✓ | 0.0088 |
  | rocket | 0.9999993 | rocket 0.9916 ✓ | 0.0116 |
  | motorcycle | 0.9999985 | motorcycle 0.9993 ✓ | 0.0138 |

## M4f: DETR detection and masks (done)

- **Driver:** `detector-detr@1`'s `DriverContract` (`detector`: in `image` rank 4; out `boxes` and `classLogits` rank 3,
  optional `masks` rank 4), checked with `preprocess.image` (`NCHW`), a `detrSigmoid` `postprocess` (with `masks`
  exactly when the graph binds them) and `labels` before anything loads; the outputs must be `[1, Q, 4]`, `[1, Q, C]`
  (C ≤ 91 under `cocoId`) and `[1, Q, h, w]`. `DetrDetectorDriver` in `Registry.vision` loads the same
  `ObjectDetectorModel` as YOLOX; `ObjectDetector`'s `Configuration.Head` gains `.detr` (`DetrHead`), not a second actor.
- **Manifest:** `detrSigmoid.selection` is typed (`topKPairs`, the default), replacing the export's `x-selection`; the
  export drops `activation` (implied by the kind), writes `masks: {logitThreshold: 0}` (was `{output, stride}`, both
  implied by the graph), names `labels: {set: coco-91}` only (no `labels.json`), and records provenance refs as
  strings. Both bundles were re-exported and `check_rfdetr.py` re-run on them (all bars pass, unchanged numbers).
- **Postprocess:** sigmoid of every logit; the best `maxDetections` labelled (query, class) pairs strictly above the
  threshold, ties by flat index; no NMS. Boxes through `DetectionBoxes`. Masks: the query's plane resampled to the
  source with `DepthResampler`'s taps scaled to the plane (bilinear at pixel centres, `align_corners=False`), strictly
  above the logit threshold, evaluated only over the plane cells above it (exact: a bilinear value never exceeds its
  four cells); `DetectionMask` stores the foreground's bounds and one bit per pixel.
- **Gate** (`ObjectDetectorDetrGateTests`, M5 Max, macOS 27), per variant, six cases, the reference's input tensor on
  the GPU against the reference's raw outputs through the same head (which itself reproduces upstream `PostProcess`'s
  detections: scores within 6e-8, boxes within 6e-7):

  | Variant | Detections (6 cases) | Min box IoU | Max \|Δscore\| | Min mask IoU | Footprint (peak) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | rf-detr-nano | 54 of 54 matched | 0.999994 | 4.4e-6 | — | 969 MiB |
  | rf-detr-seg-nano | 47 of 47 matched | 0.999997 | 2.4e-6 | 1.000000 | 1326 MiB |

  Repeats are bit-identical. Reported, not gated: the inputs rounded to 8-bit images and detected from `ImageInput`
  (min IoU 0.986–0.999998, scores within 0.014, mask IoU ≥ 0.985, one borderline extra or missing detection on
  bicycle-parade-285); and the source photos (1596 × 1072 to 3840 × 2560) end to end against the reference mapped to the
  same source: min IoU 0.65–0.99, scores within 0.17, up to six unmatched on burnside-irt (seg-nano), because Kit
  stretches with vImage and upstream with torchvision's bilinear without antialiasing. A `torchBilinear` filter
  would close that gap.
